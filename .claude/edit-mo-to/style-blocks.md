# Reusable style blocks (client-free)

Accepted blocks from past jobs, with client details replaced by `[placeholders]`. Client-specific notes live in `clients/` on this computer only; they are not in the public repo.

## Claymation (accepted on a two-character podcast ad)
- **STYLE & AUDIO block,** appended in full to every clip: "STYLE & AUDIO: Handmade stop-motion claymation, sculpted clay characters with visible fingerprints, slightly stepped, jittery stop-motion movement, [set, e.g. warm amber podcast studio], shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16. Characters keep exactly the look, printed labels, and price tags of the attached references. AUDIO: use only the attached audio file as the soundtrack and dialogue; do not generate any other speech, voices, music, or sounds. In the attached audio, the [voice A description, e.g. smug, brisk, slightly nasal mid-high voice] is the [Character A] and the [voice B description, e.g. calm, warm, lower-pitched, steady voice] is the [Character B]; keep these two voices exactly the same in every clip. Lip-sync each character's mouth precisely to its own voice; only the speaking character's mouth moves, and during cutaways the audio continues as voice-over. No subtitles, no captions, no watermarks, no on-screen text except the labels and price tags printed on the characters."
- **B-roll style line:** "Cinematic handmade claymation B-roll, richly detailed miniature set, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, visible fingerprints and tool marks, vertical 9:16, silent, no text."

## Pixar-style story (accepted on a 40s apparel ad)
- **Structure:** hook 0–5s, body 5–33s, CTA 33–40s. Keyframes S1–S9, then Seedance 2.5 clips C1–C9.
- **Character sheet:** body type, hair, wardrobe down to the buttons (e.g. "white dress shirt with cream buttons, no tie, collar open, grey trousers"), so story details read on screen.
- **Props never get faces** (e.g. a competing product shown as a plain cobalt-blue object).
- **Hero set for the product and CTA shots:** dark steel podium, cyan rim light, navy honeycomb backdrop, moving sparks, and a plate with the offer (e.g. "90 DAYS RISK FREE"); suit the set to the audience.
- **Audio:** warm narrator matched to the audience (e.g. late-30s baritone, animated-film storyteller); whimsical orchestral music around 118 BPM, made in 18s Suno parts.
- **Claims:** stay within appearance claims; no medical or weight-loss claims unless the brief verifies them.

## Product ad (accepted on a 10–20s shoe ad)
- **Format:** 10–20s, 2–4s clips, every clip a different angle, following the inspiration videos.
- **Offer 2–3 moods,** e.g. Elegant (black and gold marble), Bold Urban Night (neon, wet asphalt), Warm Heritage (terracotta and walnut).
- **Faceless models** (legs, hands, torso). One-word descriptor per shot (Refined / Commanding / Timeless). Include a comfort or feature shot.
- **Logo in one clip;** overlay the real logo file if the generated one warps.
- **Claims:** avoid anything unverified (handmade, genuine leather, Italian, weightless).
