## Method
- **Storyboard (`mode: storyboard`):** character sheets (CHAR-n) only when the client gave none; then one beat per shot in `storyboard.json`: keyframe `prompt` (`"kind": "keyframe"`), `line`, shot size, characters (who is left/right), set, and its role: `talk` (the speaker says the line) or `reaction` (the listener, mouth closed, for an overlay). Plan the shot sizes along the tension (wide at setup, tighter as it rises) and a change every 3–5 s. Flow prompts stay short-ish (Subject / Setting / Action / Camera / Style); long lock blocks get policy-blocked.
- **Locks:** one voice lock per character, written once in full in `locks` under its own name (`voice_<name>`) and listed on every clip that character speaks in; the shared `voice` lock says "Each character keeps exactly the same voice in every clip; only the speaking character's voice is heard; no music." A set lock per location and a look lock (skin, hair, outfit, grade per scene; never blurry or smeared).
- **Talking clips:** one speaker per clip, mouth moving only for that character. Dialogue verbatim in second windows (`0.0–1.8s: he says: "…"`) + "Mouth movement matches this pacing exactly" (`pacing`). Speaking starts in the first moment, no dead air. Shortest length the model allows that covers the line (credits); a long line may span two shots, cut between words.
- **Reaction clips:** the listener only, mouth closed, reacting to the named line (frown, raised brow, nod), same set, side and background as their talking shots, about 2–4 s, no dialogue. One or two per exchange where a J/L cut helps, never more than needed (credits).
- **Realistic look:** medium and wide shots attach the avatar/establishing frame so both characters stay in frame; close-ups attach only the speaker's sheet + their background plate and say "looking off-camera at <other character>, never at the camera".
- **Cartoon look:** keyframes and clips are **16:9 landscape**, faces and action in the centre 70% of the width ("horizontal 16:9 frame, subject centred" in every prompt). Make `STYLE-REF.jpg` from 3–4 caption-free reference frames and attach it last as "style reference only: its people are different people; never copy their faces, skin tones, hair or rooms". After each character's sheet attach a face crop (`FACE-<NAME>.jpg`, labels cropped off) with an `Identity:` line, and a hair lock; once a keyframe shows the hair right, crop `HEAD-<NAME>.jpg` and attach it to every later shot of that character. The `style_bible` describes the reference look (line weight, shadows, eyes, palette, backgrounds) with negatives for the wrong look, plus "No text, letters, subtitles, captions, logos or watermarks anywhere" as `notext`.
- **Edit plan:** clips in script order, 1.0x, own audio (`"mute": false`), no `audio_lead`, cuts in the pauses per the Drama pacing rule, soft Dissolve (~5 f) on same-shot jump cuts, **no zoom or scale-up on any row**. **Reaction overlays:** a reaction clip goes on an overlay track above the speaker's row, muted, over the middle or end of the line (never over its first word), and the next speaker's clip may start under the last ~0.3 s of the reaction. Move overlays and SFX with every cut. SFX for script actions and tension. `music: null`. Realistic: full-frame 9:16 rows. Cartoon panel: the frame PNG (textured background, title baked in, transparent panel window at y 620–1505) is composited onto every 16:9 clip with ffmpeg before the build (`clips_panel/C-n.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, own audio kept) and placed at scale 1.0 on the main track; a blocked or missing shot gets a labelled slate of the same length (`C-n_MISSING.mp4`).
- **Before hand-back:** transcribe each line as heard (its pieces joined) and diff it against the script; list fillers, stutters and wrong words with the clip to regenerate or the cut to make.
